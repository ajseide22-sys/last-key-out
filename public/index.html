Last Key Out

You wake up inside a pitch-black manor with nothing but a flashlight. Solve its puzzles for keys, crack open safes stuffed with cash, and find the four numbers that open the hidden way out. Things live here. They hunt your light, and some wait in the dark to jump out. Get caught and you lose every dollar you're carrying. Escape with your money and spend it on outfits.

How it works
  • Puzzles (a lever panel, a clock, a piano, a symbol box) each give the crew a key. Clues are written on the walls. A few keys are hidden in furniture too.
  • Each key opens one safe. Safes hold cash, and two of them also hold a number for the way out. Furniture has loose cash too.
  • If a monster catches you, you lose all the money you're carrying and get locked in the jail until a friend picks the lock.
  • Two numbers are on the walls and two are in safes. Find the bookshelf with the red book sticking out and enter all four to open the secret passage. Walk in to escape and keep your money.
